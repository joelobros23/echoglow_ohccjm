# EchoGlow - DLSHUPU

## Unveiling Knowledge, Igniting Collaboration, Illuminating Futures

**EchoGlow - DLSHUPU** is not just another web application; it's a vibrant, dynamic digital ecosystem meticulously crafted to serve as the premier academic and collaborative hub for the esteemed community of De La Salle Health Sciences Institute and Medical Center - University of Perpetual Help System DALTA (DLSHUPU). ## 🌟 About the Project: The Genesis of EchoGlow

In the ever-evolving landscape of modern academia, the need for a robust, intuitive, and interconnected platform has become paramount. Students, faculty, researchers, and alumni often find themselves navigating disparate systems, leading to fragmented information, missed collaborative opportunities, and a diluted sense of community. EchoGlow - DLSHUPU was conceived to bridge these gaps, creating a unified digital space where every idea, every project, and every achievement can find its voice and shine brightly.

Our mission is simple yet profound: **To empower every member of the DLSHUPU community to share, collaborate, learn, and grow without boundaries.**

EchoGlow is built on the philosophy that collective intelligence and open communication are the catalysts for groundbreaking discoveries and transformative education. It's a place where a student's groundbreaking research can be showcased to the world, where faculty members can seamlessly collaborate on interdisciplinary projects, and where the rich tapestry of DLSHUPU's intellectual capital is preserved, amplified, and made accessible to all who seek it.

**Key Objectives:**

*   **Centralize Knowledge:** Provide a single, comprehensive repository for all academic resources, research, and institutional information.
*   **Foster Collaboration:** Create intuitive tools and spaces that encourage seamless interaction and teamwork among peers, mentors, and departments.
*   **Amplify Achievements:** Offer a sophisticated platform for showcasing projects, publications, and professional milestones, boosting visibility and recognition.
*   **Cultivate Community:** Strengthen the bonds within the DLSHUPU family, connecting current members with alumni and industry professionals.
*   **Drive Innovation:** Serve as a launchpad for new ideas, discussions, and interdisciplinary initiatives that push the boundaries of healthcare and education.

EchoGlow is more than a platform; it's a testament to DLSHUPU's commitment to excellence, innovation, and community.

## ✨ Key Features: A Glimpse into EchoGlow's Capabilities

EchoGlow is packed with a comprehensive suite of features designed to enhance every facet of academic and professional life at DLSHUPU. Crafted with a user-centric approach, each module is intuitive, powerful, and deeply integrated to provide a seamless experience.

### 👥 **1. Comprehensive User Management & Dynamic Profiles**

*   **Secure Authentication & Authorization:** Leveraging the industry-standard `Devise` gem, EchoGlow ensures robust login security. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) meticulously defines permissions for `Students`, `Faculty/Staff`, `Researchers`, `Alumni`, and `Administrators`, ensuring appropriate access to content and functionalities.
*   **Rich User Profiles:** Far beyond basic contact information, user profiles on EchoGlow are a digital representation of an individual's academic and professional journey.
    *   **Academic Credentials:** Showcase degrees, specializations, affiliations, and current academic standing.
    *   **Research Interests & Expertise:** Tag and categorize specific areas of interest, making it easier for collaborators to find each other.
    *   **Skills Matrix:** List proficiency in various technical, clinical, and soft skills.
    *   **Portfolio & Publications:** Link to external portfolios, upload research papers, project reports, and publications.
    *   **Contact & Social Links:** Securely share professional contact details and links to professional social media profiles (LinkedIn, ORCID, ResearchGate).
    *   **Activity Feed:** A personalized feed displaying recent contributions, collaborations, and interactions within the platform.
*   **Directory & Search:** An intelligent directory allowing users to search for peers, faculty, or alumni based on various criteria like name, department, research interests, or skills.

### 📚 **2. Project & Research Showcase Hub**

*   **Dedicated Project Pages:** Every project, thesis, dissertation, or research initiative gets its own comprehensive page.
    *   **Detailed Overviews:** Abstract, objectives, methodology, results, and conclusions.
    *   **Team Collaboration:** List all contributing members with links to their profiles, fostering recognition and networking.
    *   **Resource & Media Attachments:** Upload supporting documents (PDFs, PPTs), images, videos, and external links relevant to the project. `Active Storage` handles secure and efficient file management.
    *   **Version Control (Basic):** Track different versions of uploaded documents with clear timestamps and change notes, ensuring integrity and auditability.
    *   **Project Status Tracking:** Mark projects as `In Progress`, `Completed`, `Published`, or `Seeking Collaborators`.
*   **Categorization & Tagging:** Intuitive tagging and categorization system (e.g., "Public Health," "Pharmacology," "Medical Ethics") to improve discoverability and organization.
*   **Advanced Search & Filtering:** Powerful search capabilities allow users to find projects based on keywords, authors, departments, publication dates, and more.
*   **Peer Review & Feedback:** Optional modules for internal peer review processes on submitted research, facilitating constructive criticism and quality improvement.

### 🤝 **3. Collaborative Workspaces & Team Management**

*   **Private & Public Workspaces:** Users can create dedicated workspaces for specific courses, research groups, departmental initiatives, or student organizations. Workspaces can be set as `Public` (viewable by all DLSHUPU members) or `Private` (invite-only).
*   **Integrated Discussion Boards:** Each workspace features its own forum for asynchronous discussions, Q&A sessions, and idea brainstorming, replacing fragmented email chains.
*   **Shared File Repository:** A secure space within each workspace for team members to upload, share, and manage project-related files and documents.
*   **Lightweight Task Management:** Implement a simple task list or Kanban-style board within workspaces to help teams organize tasks, assign responsibilities, and track progress.
*   **Meeting Scheduler:** Basic integration for scheduling virtual or in-person meetings, potentially linking with external calendar services.

### 📖 **4. Knowledge Base & Resource Hub**

*   **Curated Content Library:** A centralized repository for academic articles, lecture notes, study guides, tutorials, and institutional policies, curated and approved by faculty and administrators.
*   **Community Contributions:** Empower students and faculty to contribute to the knowledge base, with a moderation pipeline to ensure quality and accuracy.
*   **Resource Tagging & Categorization:** Organize resources by subject, course, department, or topic for easy navigation.
*   **Version History for Resources:** Maintain a history of changes for important documents and articles.
*   **"Ask an Expert" Feature:** A dedicated section where users can post specific questions to be answered by designated faculty or subject matter experts.

### 🗓️ **5. Event Management & Announcements**

*   **Dynamic Event Calendar:** A comprehensive calendar showcasing university-wide events, academic seminars, workshops, thesis defenses, examination schedules, and application deadlines.
*   **Event Creation & Management:** Authorized users (faculty, club organizers, administrators) can create, publish, and manage events, including details like venue, date, time, description, and speaker information.
*   **RSVP Functionality:** Users can RSVP to events, allowing organizers to gauge attendance and send targeted updates.
*   **Automated Reminders:** Event participants receive automated email or in-app notifications before the event.
*   **University-Wide Announcements:** A dedicated section for official institutional announcements, news, and urgent alerts, ensuring critical information reaches the entire community promptly.

### 💬 **6. Interactive Forums & Q&A Sections**

*   **General Discussion Forums:** Open forums for discussions on various topics, from academic challenges to campus life.
*   **Subject-Specific Q&A:** Dedicated sections for students to post questions related to specific courses or subjects, where faculty and knowledgeable peers can provide answers.
*   **Gamification Elements:** Features like upvoting/downvoting for questions and answers, "best answer" selection, and reputation points to encourage high-quality contributions and engagement.
*   **Rich Text Editor:** Empower users to craft detailed posts with formatting, images, and code snippets.

### 🌐 **7. Networking & Mentorship Program**

*   **Alumni Network Integration:** Connect current students and faculty with the vast network of DLSHUPU alumni, fostering professional relationships and mentorship opportunities.
*   **Mentor Matching System:** An intelligent system that suggests potential mentors based on academic background, research interests, skills, and career goals.
*   **Private Messaging:** Secure in-app messaging system for one-on-one communication, facilitating mentorship interactions and collaborative discussions.
*   **Professional Endorsements:** Allow users to endorse each other's skills and expertise, building credible online profiles.

### ⚙️ **8. Robust Admin Dashboard & Analytics**

*   **Comprehensive User Management:** Administrators have full control over user accounts, including creation, editing, deletion, password resets, and role assignment.
*   **Content Moderation:** Tools to review, approve, or reject user-generated content (e.g., forum posts, knowledge base contributions, project descriptions) to maintain platform integrity and quality.
*   **Site-Wide Configuration:** Manage global settings, categories, tags, announcement banners, and email templates.
*   **Basic Analytics & Reporting:** Gain insights into user activity, popular content, engagement metrics, and system performance.
*   **Audit Logs:** Track administrative actions and significant system events for security and compliance.

### 📱 **9. Responsive Design & Accessibility**

*   **Mobile-First Approach:** EchoGlow is built with a responsive design philosophy, ensuring a seamless and intuitive experience across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
*   **Accessibility Standards:** Adherence to WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) principles to make the platform usable for individuals with disabilities, ensuring inclusivity.

### 📧 **10. Intelligent Notifications System**

*   **Real-time Alerts:** Users receive instant notifications for new messages, replies to their posts, project updates, event reminders, and relevant announcements.
*   **Configurable Preferences:** Users can customize their notification settings, choosing how and when they receive alerts (e.g., in-app, email).
*   **Digest Emails:** Optional daily or weekly summary emails highlighting key activities and unread notifications.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture Overview

EchoGlow is engineered with a modern, scalable, and maintainable technology stack, primarily leveraging the power of Ruby on Rails.

*   **Backend Framework:** **Ruby on Rails 7.x** - The backbone of the application, providing a robust MVC architecture, convention-over-configuration, and a wealth of powerful gems.
*   **Programming Language:** **Ruby 3.x** - Chosen for its elegance, expressiveness, and strong community support.
*   **Database:** **PostgreSQL** - A powerful, reliable, open-source object-relational database system, ideal for handling complex academic data relationships and ensuring data integrity.
*   **Frontend Interactivity:** **Hotwire (Turbo & Stimulus.js)** - For delivering a snappy, reactive user experience akin to single-page applications, but with the simplicity and productivity of traditional server-side rendering. This minimizes JavaScript complexity while maximizing user engagement.
*   **Asset Management:** **jsbundling-rails** (with ESBuild) & **cssbundling-rails** (with Tailwind CSS/Sass) - For efficient compilation and bundling of JavaScript and CSS assets.
*   **File Uploads:** **Active Storage** - Rails' integrated solution for uploading files to cloud storage services (e.g., Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, Microsoft Azure Storage) or directly to disk.
*   **Background Processing:** **GoodJob** (or Sidekiq) - For handling asynchronous tasks such as sending notification emails, processing large data imports, or generating reports, ensuring the main application remains responsive.
*   **Authentication:** **Devise** - A flexible authentication solution for Rails.
*   **Authorization:** **Pundit** (or CanCanCan) - For managing granular role-based permissions throughout the application.
*   **Testing:** **RSpec** - A behavior-driven development framework for writing comprehensive unit, integration, and feature tests.
*   **Containerization (Deployment Consideration):** **Docker** - For creating isolated, portable environments for development, testing, and production, ensuring consistency across different stages.

This stack provides a foundation that is not only powerful and efficient but also highly extensible, allowing for continuous growth and integration of future technologies.

## 🚀 Installation: Setting Up EchoGlow Locally

Embark on your journey with EchoGlow by setting up the application on your local machine. Follow these detailed instructions to get the development environment up and running.

### **1. Prerequisites**

Ensure you have the following software installed on your system. Specific versions are recommended for optimal compatibility:

*   **Ruby:** Version `3.2.2` or newer.
    *   *Recommendation:* Use `rbenv` or `RVM` for managing Ruby versions.
*   **Ruby on Rails:** Version `7.0.8` or newer.
    *   Install with: `gem install rails`
*   **PostgreSQL:** Version `14.x` or newer.
    *   Ensure the PostgreSQL server is running.
    *   Make sure you have a PostgreSQL user with appropriate permissions (e.g., `postgres` user or a dedicated user for `echoglow`).
*   **Node.js:** LTS version `18.x` or newer.
    *   *Recommendation:* Use `nvm` for managing Node.js versions.
*   **Yarn:** Version `1.22.x` or `npm` (if preferred).
    *   Install Yarn with: `npm install --global yarn`
*   **Git:** Latest version.

### **2. Getting Started**

1.  **Clone the repository:**
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/DLSHUPU/echoglow.git
    cd echoglow
    ```

### **3. Database Setup**

EchoGlow uses PostgreSQL. You'll need to configure your database connection and create the necessary databases.

1.  **Configure `database.yml`:**
    Open `config/database.yml`. By default, it's configured for PostgreSQL. You might need to adjust the `username` and `password` for your local PostgreSQL setup, especially for the `development` and `test` environments.

    *Example `config/database.yml` Snippet (adjust as needed):*
    ```yaml
    default: &default
      adapter: postgresql
      encoding: unicode
      pool: <%= ENV.fetch("RAILS_MAX_THREADS") { 5 } %>

    development:
      <<: *default
      database: echoglow_development
      username: your_pg_username # e.g., postgres
      password: your_pg_password # if you have one

    test:
      <<: *default
      database: echoglow_test
      username: your_pg_username
      password: your_pg_password
    ```

2.  **Create and Migrate the Database:**
    ```bash
    rails db:create  # Creates echoglow_development and echoglow_test databases
    rails db:migrate # Runs database migrations to set up table schemas
    ```

3.  **Seed Initial Data (Optional but Recommended):**
    To populate your database with some initial sample data (e.g., an admin user, sample projects, or categories), run the seed file:
    ```bash
    rails db:seed
    ```
    *Note:* Check `db/seeds.rb` for details on what data will be created. It usually includes an initial admin user (e.g., `admin@dlshupu.edu.ph` with password `password`) for development purposes. **Change this in production!**

### **4. Install Dependencies**

1.  **Ruby Gems:**
    Install all required Ruby gems:
    ```bash
    bundle install
    ```

2.  **JavaScript Packages:**
    Install frontend JavaScript packages using Yarn (or npm):
    ```bash
    yarn install
    # or npm install
    ```

### **5. Environment Variables**

EchoGlow uses environment variables for sensitive configurations (e.g., secret keys, API credentials, mailer settings). We recommend using `dotenv-rails` for local development.

1.  **Create `.env` file:**
    Duplicate the provided example environment file:
    ```bash
    cp .env.example .env
    ```

2.  **Edit `.env`:**
    Open the newly created `.env` file and fill in the necessary values. At minimum, ensure `SECRET_KEY_BASE` is set. You can generate a new one with `rails secret`.
    *Example `.env` content:*
    ```
    RAILS_ENV=development
    SECRET_KEY_BASE=your_long_random_secret_key_generated_by_rails_secret

    # Example: Mailer Configuration (for Devise emails, notifications)
    MAILER_SENDER=no-reply@echoglow.dlshupu.edu.ph
    SMTP_ADDRESS=smtp.mailtrap.io # or your actual SMTP server
    SMTP_PORT=2525
    SMTP_USERNAME=your_smtp_username
    SMTP_PASSWORD=your_smtp_password
    SMTP_DOMAIN=example.com

    # Example: Cloudinary (if using for Active Storage)
    CLOUDINARY_URL=cloudinary://YOUR_API_KEY:YOUR_API_SECRET@YOUR_CLOUD_NAME

    # Add other environment variables as needed (e.g., API keys for integrations)
    ```
    **Important:** Never commit your `.env` file to version control. It's already in `.gitignore`.

### **6. Run the Application**

EchoGlow uses `jsbundling-rails` and `cssbundling-rails`, which means you need to run both the Rails server and the frontend build process concurrently.

1.  **Using `bin/dev` (Recommended):**
    If your `Procfile.dev` is configured (which it usually is in Rails 7), you can use `bin/dev` to start both the Rails server and the frontend watcher.
    ```bash
    bin/dev
    ```
    This command will typically start:
    *   The Rails web server (`rails s`)
    *   The JavaScript bundler (`yarn build --watch` or `npm run build --watch`)
    *   The CSS bundler (`yarn build:css --watch` or `npm run build:css --watch`)

2.  **Manual Start (Alternative):**
    If `bin/dev` doesn't work or you prefer manual control, open three separate terminal tabs:
    *   **Tab 1 (Rails Server):**
        ```bash
        rails server
        ```
    *   **Tab 2 (JavaScript Bundler):**
        ```bash
        yarn build --watch
        # or npm run build --watch
        ```
    *   **Tab 3 (CSS Bundler):**
        ```bash
        yarn build:css --watch
        # or npm run build:css --watch
        ```

Once the servers are running, open your web browser and navigate to: `http://localhost:3000`

### **7. Running Tests**

It's good practice to ensure everything is working as expected by running the test suite.

```bash
bundle exec rspec # If using RSpec (default for EchoGlow)
# or
rails test # If using Minitest
```

## 🤝 Contributing: Join the EchoGlow Initiative!

EchoGlow - DLSHUPU is an open-source project driven by the collaborative spirit of the DLSHUPU community. We welcome and encourage contributions from students, faculty, alumni, and passionate developers who wish to help us build a more robust, feature-rich, and impactful platform. Your ideas, bug reports, code improvements, and documentation enhancements are invaluable!

### **Philosophy**

Our project thrives on open collaboration, respect, and a shared commitment to enhancing the academic experience at DLSHUPU. We believe that diverse perspectives lead to better solutions and a stronger community.

### **How to Contribute**

1.  **Fork the Repository:** Start by forking the `DLSHUPU/echoglow` repository on GitHub to your personal account.
2.  **Create a Feature Branch:** Always create a new branch for your specific feature or bug fix.
    ```bash
    git checkout main
    git pull origin main # Ensure your main branch is up-to-date
    git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name-or-bugfix-description
    ```
    *Naming Convention:* Use descriptive names like `feature/add-dark-mode` or `fix/project-upload-error`.
3.  **Make Your Changes:** Implement your feature, fix the bug, or improve documentation.
    *   **Follow Style Guides:** Adhere to Ruby/Rails best practices and the existing code style. We use `RuboCop` for code linting; ensure your code passes its checks.
    *   **Write Tests:** For any new features or bug fixes, please write corresponding tests (`RSpec` is preferred). This ensures stability and prevents regressions.
    *   **Update Documentation:** If your changes affect user-facing functionality or core logic, update the relevant documentation (e.g., `README.md`, inline comments).
4.  **Run Tests:** Before committing, ensure all existing tests pass, and your new tests cover your changes.
    ```bash
    bundle exec rspec
    ```
5.  **Commit Your Changes:** Write clear, concise, and descriptive commit messages. We encourage [Semantic Commit Messages](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/) to help track changes effectively.
    *Example:*
    ```
    git commit -m "feat: Add user profile picture upload via Active Storage"
    ```
6.  **Push to Your Fork:**
    ```bash
    git push origin feature/your-feature-name-or-bugfix-description
    ```
7.  **Open a Pull Request (PR):**
    *   Go to the original `DLSHUPU/echoglow` repository on GitHub.
    *   You should see a prompt to create a new Pull Request from your pushed branch.
    *   **Provide a Detailed Description:** Clearly explain the purpose of your PR, what problem it solves, how it was implemented, and any relevant screenshots or steps to reproduce.
    *   **Reference Issues:** Link to any relevant GitHub Issues your PR addresses (e.g., `Closes #123`, `Fixes #456`).
    *   **Request Review:** Our core team will review your PR, provide feedback, and work with you to get it merged.

### **Contribution Guidelines**

*   **Reporting Bugs:**
    *   Before opening a new issue, please check if a similar bug has already been reported.
    *   Provide a clear and concise description of the bug.
    *   Include steps to reproduce the bug.
    *   Describe the expected behavior and what actually happened.
    *   Specify your operating system, browser, Ruby/Rails versions, and any relevant error messages.
*   **Suggesting Enhancements:**
    *   Clearly describe the feature or enhancement you're suggesting.
    *   Explain its use case and why you believe it would be valuable to EchoGlow and the DLSHUPU community.
    *   Consider providing mock-ups or examples if applicable.
*   **Code Contributions:**
    *   Keep your Pull Requests focused on a single feature or bug fix.
    *   Ensure your code is clean, readable, and well-commented where necessary.
    *   Avoid introducing new external dependencies unless absolutely necessary and justified.
    *   Address all feedback from code reviews promptly and professionally.

### **Code of Conduct**

EchoGlow is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone. We expect all contributors to adhere to our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) (a separate file usually linked here). In essence: be respectful, be inclusive, and foster a positive environment.

### **Getting Help**

If you have questions, encounter issues, or need guidance while contributing, please:

*   Open an issue on GitHub describing your question or problem.
*   Reach out to the core development team (contact details might be provided in `CONTRIBUTING.md` or here).

## 🗺️ Roadmap & Future Enhancements (Vision 2025)

EchoGlow is a living project, continuously evolving to meet the dynamic needs of the DLSHUPU community. Our roadmap outlines both immediate priorities and long-term aspirations.

### **Short-Term Goals (Next 6-12 Months)**
